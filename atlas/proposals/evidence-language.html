Why it connects

Mulholland Drive is a 2001 mystery film written and directed by David Lynch. Its narrative is characterized by surrealist and neo-noir elements, and follows an aspiring actress newly arrived in Los Angeles, where she befriends a car crash victim who is suffering from amnesia.
