The circled take

TYPE HAND · SIGNAL BALLOT · SOURCE RECORD · STRENGTH 0.55 FLAT · ONE MARK, THREE SURFACES

A director's ballot is a maker circling ten takes out of all of cinema — esteem on the record, influence unclaimed. It rides the fifth type, hand, because the connective tissue is a named person, which is hand's own grammar; it is a record, because a published ballot is checkable the way a credit is; and it draws as a chinagraph circle at the chosen end of the line — the mark an editor greases onto a workprint, the mark this project already gives the reader's own hand in gold. Two hands, one vocabulary: the reader's ring sits on the disc, the maker's circle sits on the edge.

A · On the radial map

Parasite, and the one door its maker's ballot opens

Two of Parasite's doors are argued readings — they draw dashed, in their type's colour, stronger and nearer than the ballot. The ballot is the third door: a solid hand-hue line at flat strength 0.55, labelled NAMES IT, with the grease circle at the chosen end. Direction is legible at rest with no arrowhead: the circle sits where the choosing landed, and the hand that chose is at the other end of the line. At most one ballot door per map — the rest of a film's ballots live in the panel's ledger, because thirteen doors saying "someone famous chose this" is a leaderboard wearing a map's clothes.

B · The mark, at 6×

A pencil, not a compass

The one hand-drawn mark in a machine-ruled interface is the one made by a hand — that is what it teaches, and why nothing else may borrow it. It marks the edge's end, never the film: a disc or cell carries no esteem badge (rule 1), so a film named on thirteen ballots looks identical to one named on none until a single edge is being read. It rhymes with the reader's gold loved ring — chinagraph in both cases; the reader marks the print, the maker marks the line.

Where it may not appear: the constellation, ever. At field scale thirteen circles converging on Citizen Kane draw a halo of esteem — fame made visible, rule 1 broken by accumulation. In the sky a ballot is an ordinary hand line at every zoom.

Its only differentiators are the glyph (the same circle, 9px, inline) and the hand-hue keyline every hand edge already gets.
